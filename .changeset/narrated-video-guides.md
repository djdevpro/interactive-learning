The player can show a guide like a narrated video. `frame="browser"` puts the video in a macOS browser window with the page address in its title bar, and the screen gets room around it (`--il-screen-padding`). A step can zoom toward its click (`zoom`), and the camera frames each step, lifting action that sits low on screen toward the middle. A story line can carry its voice (`audio`), played in step with the guide behind a speaker button (`muted`), and its `words` with their timing, shown over the screen as captions lit as they are said. The arrow and the markers shrink with a narrow player, and rounded corners stay clean on every screen. The guide format, and the recorder that validates it, keep `zoom`, `audio` and `words`.
